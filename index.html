Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026  •  Android app

Nova Slide is an offline puzzle game. This Privacy Policy describes the game information stored on your device, how to remove it, and how to contact the developer.

1. Overview

Nova Slide (package name: com.novaslide.puzzle) is an offline sliding-number puzzle game. The game does not require account registration or sign-in. This policy explains how information is handled in the current version of the game.

2. Information and game data

The app does not ask you to provide your name, phone number, email address, precise location, photos, or contacts. To support the game, it stores certain non-personal gameplay information in Android SharedPreferences on your device: your sound preference, best move scores, saved puzzle difficulty, tile arrangement, restart arrangement, move count, and elapsed play time.

3. How we use this information

Locally stored game data is used to show best scores, remember whether sound is on or off, and allow you to resume an unfinished puzzle. Nova Slide does not send this gameplay information to the developer and does not operate a server for gameplay information.

4. Advertising, analytics, sharing, and network access

The current version of Nova Slide is designed to work offline and does not include in-app advertisements, analytics SDKs, or tracking SDKs. The developer does not collect or share personal information through the app. There is no in-app account system. If future versions add any such services, this policy will be updated before or when those changes are introduced.

5. Android backup and security

Android may back up some app data, including SharedPreferences, depending on the device, Android version, and the user's system backup settings. Such operating-system backups are controlled by Android and the user's backup provider, not by Nova Slide. Locally stored preferences are held in the app's private storage area, subject to the device's normal Android security protections. Nova Slide does not transmit gameplay data to its own servers.

6. Permissions and sound

The game does not need access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photos. Sound effects are generated locally through the device audio output. Sound can be switched on or off from the main menu.

7. Data retention and deletion

Game progress, best scores, and settings remain on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall Nova Slide, subject to any copies stored by your device's backup service. Starting a new game replaces the saved unfinished puzzle; it does not erase best scores. To delete locally stored game information, open Android Settings > Apps > Nova Slide > Storage & cache > Clear storage (wording may differ by device), or uninstall the app. If Android backup is enabled, manage backup copies through your Android or Google backup settings.

8. Children's privacy

Nova Slide does not knowingly request, collect, or transmit personal information from children or other users. The app offers offline gameplay without accounts, advertisements, or social features.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's features or data practices change. The revised policy will be posted on this page and its Last updated date will be changed.
